# Surface brief — CodeLens demo page (static/index.html)

Scope: single-page local demo UI served by stdlib server. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience/job: hackathon judges (grasp the verified-refs story in under two minutes) and developers (run scan → analyze → ask on real repos).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a surveyor's plat of a live codebase analysis — every AI claim staked to exact file:line coordinates, verified monuments inked, rejected refs stamped void. It refuses the grey-dashboard default and the chat-window costume; trust is spatial, not conversational.

OWN-WORLD: Drafting-paper ground with a hairline survey grid; iron blue-black ink; survey-orange reserved for pins, the live chainage, and primary actions; green/amber/red doing only state jobs (verified/inferred/rejected). System grotesk for prose and headings, ui-monospace for coordinates and code. 1px rules, tick marks, drawn monument badges; cards flat with 1px ink borders, one soft offset shadow reserved for the pinned result sheet.

STORY: A judge sees within seconds what the repo is, then staked answers with coordinates and visibly voided rejections. A developer runs the three instruments, reads answers with leader lines to quoted evidence, and copies coordinates into their editor.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Drawing-frame header (sheet mark, title, repo path field, local-model tag). One ruled instrument band: Scan / Analyze / Ask plus question field, Ask primary. Below, the result sheet: answer prose at reading measure, outboard rail of coordinate callouts with leader lines, monuments green, voids red-stamped with reasons. Chainage timer ticks honestly during model waits. Raises carried: margin keys on every ref; VERIFIED/VOID/INFERRED grounds; model timings as surveyed duration bars; focused result swells while the rest dim; waiting state ticks through a chainage readout; state readable by line-weight alone.

FORM: Candidate 4 of 7 (proof-desk galley, evidence ledger, card catalog, surveyor's plat, exhibit board, observatory log, flight strips). Seed key ec390e58.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
